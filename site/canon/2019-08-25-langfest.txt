2019 -- LangFest
----------------
Marc Okrand attended.

LangFest 2019 took place at Concordia University in Montreal, Canada, over the
weekend August 23--25, 2019. Keynote speakers where David J. Peterson and Marc
Okrand, who both also held workshop events during the weekend.

Marc Okrand's Klingon workshop (held Sunday 25th), was recorded and put on
YouTube (https://youtu.be/5G_v20YlwWc?t=3602). And one of the questions he get
from the audience and during this workshop is the following:

> **Audience member:** How do you say, for example, Borg or Vulcan?
>
> **Okrand:** Borg is {borgh}, and Vulcan is {vulqan}. {romuluS}...
> {romuluSngan}, oh, {ngan}... {ngan} is a suffix that means 'people of'. So,
> {romuluSngan}.

Interestingly enough, this is the first time that the Borg are named in
Klingon!

* {borgh} "Borg" (n) -- New word.
